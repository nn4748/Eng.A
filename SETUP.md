# Bending Test Quiz – setup

- `quiz-site/` — the website. Drag this folder onto app.netlify.com/drop.
- Results are sent to the Google Form set in `quiz-site/app.js` (top of the file);
  the form saves them in its linked Google Sheet.
- Testing tip: open `<your-site>/?reset` on a phone to clear that phone's saved attempt.
